Voluntary Mentor Step-Down

Step down from your active mentoring responsibilities while preserving your student account, profile records, and historical reviews.

What Happens When You Step Down

  • Your profile will be removed from the public Mentor Directory and cannot receive new requests.
  • Access to the Mentor Workspace and mentor tools will be suspended.
  • Your Student Workspace, career roadmaps, resume tools, and student settings remain 100% active and untouched.
  • All completed sessions, student reviews, ratings, and conversation history are fully preserved.
  • You can reactivate your approved mentor profile at any time without submitting a new application.
