The Career Journey

From learning to landing your target role.

CareerCatalyst eliminates preparation guesswork through a structured, end-to-end progression model.

01
Phase 1 • Direction Readiness Assessment

Discover Your Direction

Document your academic background, current technical competencies, and target engineering domain. The career guidance engine benchmarks your profile against company cutoffs and identifies concrete missing skill gaps before you start studying.

Deliverable: Calibrated readiness score and customized target role gap report.
02
Phase 2 • Curriculum Structured Roadmaps

Build Through Structured Roadmaps

Enroll in structured, week-by-week learning roadmaps tailored for modern tech roles. Progress topic by topic through curated documentation, code practice, and milestone deliverables instead of jumping across random tutorials.

Deliverable: 100% verified topic checklist, weekly project milestones, and deep conceptual grasp.
03
Phase 3 • Validation Resume & Assessment

Prepare Resumes & Interviews

Generate standardized, single-column technical resumes and run deterministic ATS keyword diagnostics against target job descriptions. Practice timed computer science MCQs, domain challenges, and mock technical assessments with instant feedback.

Deliverable: ATS-optimized resume benchmarked for keywords and timed screening interview confidence.
04
Phase 4 • Outreach Kanban CRM

Apply & Track Applications

Organize your job hunt on a drag-and-drop Kanban pipeline spanning Applied, Assessment, Interview, and Offer stages. Receive proactive 5-day follow-up reminders and use tailored outreach templates to keep applications moving forward.

Deliverable: Centralized outreach pipeline, scheduled follow-ups, and organized interview schedules.
05
Phase 5 • Network Mentorship & Peer Hub

Grow with Mentorship & Community

Connect with fellow student developers in peer discussion forums, showcase completed projects for community review, and book 1-on-1 mentorship sessions with verified engineering professionals for career advice and mock reviews.

Deliverable: Direct access to experienced mentors, project feedback, and career launch.
